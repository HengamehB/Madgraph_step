Methods and coverage

Tree-level production excludes loop-induced processes. The ranked rates use the narrow-width approximation. Distinct histories can overlap, so adding them can double-count a final state.

Scale choice 3 depends on external momenta. Production and explicit decay-chain integrations can use different numerical scales.

The assumed electroweak vacuum has a 125 GeV Higgs and zero new-scalar VEVs.

  • Tree-level narrow-width approximation; no decay-product acceptance or detector efficiency.
  • Loop-induced production and decay channels are excluded, including gluon fusion and Higgs loop decays in the renormalizable SM template.
  • MC errors propagate production integration only; width, scale, PDF and approximation errors are not included.
  • Different production channels and intermediate histories can overlap or interfere; rows are not an inclusive total.
  • Coverage measures supplied decay-table probabilities, not unknown loop modes or the total physical branching fraction beyond the width approximation.
  • Quarks, gluons and tau are terminal parton-level objects; this is not hadronization or tau-decay simulation.
  • Equal dynamical_scale_choice integers do not ensure equal numerical renormalization or factorization scales: production uses external parents while a decay chain uses their external daughters. This can change PDFs and running couplings independently of the resonance window.
  • Explicit decay-chain comparisons can differ through scale choices, finite resonance windows, off-shell kinematics, cuts, spin correlations or interference within retained diagrams, and numerical uncertainty; a discrepancy is not solely a resonance-window effect.
  • Tree-level MadWidth through 2-body decays; 1% adaptive target. Reported modes require BR ≥ 1e-08, without renormalizing their BRs or total widths. Unknown adaptive/body tails remain; total-width accuracy is not guaranteed.
  • x2 > u s: retained perturbative partial width 0.040563 GeV below the QCD scale; hadronization may matter.
  • x3 > d s: retained perturbative partial width 0.076991 GeV below the QCD scale; hadronization may matter.
  • x3 > s s: retained perturbative partial width 0.0020676 GeV below the QCD scale; hadronization may matter.
  • Reported decay rows omit modes below min_br; omitted_probability records their summed BR for each parent. The SLHA card and total widths retain those modes.
  • Adaptive precision and the maximum body multiplicity can leave uncomputed contributions. Their probability is unknown; reported BRs use the computed total, not a guaranteed physical total.
  • The SM electroweak vacuum and zero new-scalar VEVs are assumed; the scalar potential is not minimized. Global stability, boundedness and vacuum lifetime are not established.
