Download and extract the source archive, then open a report in your browser. Each ranked CSV contains every discovered history. A blank rate means that the production channel was not integrated. [Inputs and rerun commands](../input/README.md)

The five paper comparisons link to the generated Lagrangians, UFOs, cards and logs. Each archived input.txt preserves the settings used to calculate its results, including the original output location. The models in input/ point new runs to output/. Published manifests record checksums of the downloadable tables. [Software regression record](regression_summary.json)
